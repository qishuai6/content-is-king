# 内容为王 Core

Source article:
- [从零到百万粉丝，我做AI这三年来总结的内容创作方法论！](https://www.uisdc.com/the-viral-playbook)

## Three-Step Mapping

The source article reduces content work to three steps:
- 获取信息
- 找角度
- 创作

This skill keeps the same structure and turns each step into a separate capability inside one larger skill.

## Step 1: 获取信息

Key idea:
- Do not rely only on the topic's home domain.
- Build a wider input portfolio so the eventual angle has more range and more human depth.

Useful input buckets:
- direct facts and source materials
- history and business context
- film, comedy, narrative craft, design, psychology
- daily-life observations
- examples of human behavior, conflict, aspiration, and misunderstanding

Failure pattern:
- narrow input creates dry, same-looking content

## Step 2: 找角度

Key idea:
- the article treats angle as the highest-leverage decision
- the useful test is "情理之中，预料之外"

Operational translation:
1. identify the default take
2. reject it
3. search for overlooked contrast
4. choose the angle that is surprising without becoming fake

Good angle patterns:
- top-down view vs bottom-up reality
- public narrative vs neglected cost
- event update vs structural meaning
- common assumption vs hidden motive
- optimism story vs tension story

Failure pattern:
- first-take framing that sounds like everyone else

## Step 3: 创作

Key idea:
- execution matters less than angle selection, but bad execution can still waste a good angle
- the draft needs rhythm, not flat explanation

Operational translation:
- open with tension or reversal
- move through rising beats
- escalate with stronger proof or a sharper turn
- land on a changed understanding

Useful rhythm references named by the article:
- comedy escalation
- narrative arc
- emotional movement

Failure pattern:
- correct information delivered like a manual

## Trust and Value Floor

The article also adds non-negotiable guardrails:
- do not pretend to know what you do not know
- do not use sensitive provocation as a growth hack
- stay humble instead of superiority-signaling

## Quick Review Checklist

Before delivering work, check:
- Did the output move beyond information forwarding?
- Did the angle avoid the obvious first take?
- Did the draft create movement instead of staying flat?
- Did the piece remain trustworthy?

## Post-Publish Metrics

The article recommends judging quality with stronger signals than raw views alone:
- loyal reader count
- like-to-view ratio
- share-to-view ratio
- completion rate

Use these as feedback loops:
- high click and weak completion usually means packaging outran substance
- strong likes but weak shares usually means agreement without transmission value
- strong shares usually mean the audience gained a lens worth passing on
